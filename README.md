## 📊 Featured Analysis

### [Nike, Inc. — FY2024 Sales Performance Analysis](https://yonisv.github.io/nike-sales-analysis/)
An independent data analysis of Nike's $51.4B fiscal year 2024 performance — built in Python using Pandas and Plotly, presented as an interactive HTML dashboard.

**Key Findings:**
- Total revenue grew just 0.3% — essentially flat on a $51.4B base
- North America (-1.0%) is the only declining region despite being 43% of revenue
- APLA (+4.6%) and Greater China (+4.1%) are the real growth engines
- DTC now represents 43.5% of Nike Brand revenue — up from ~30% five years ago
- Footwear at 68.6% of revenue is a significant concentration risk
